PRIVACY

Your information deserves care.

This page summarizes current DIGITracker practices based on the app’s published product documentation. It is a working draft and needs the company’s contact and legal details before publication.

WORKING DRAFT · REVIEW BEFORE PUBLICATION

Information in DIGITracker

When you create an account, the app stores account-related information and tracker data needed to provide its features. Tracker entries may include activities, meal notes, body measurements, sleep and soreness check-ins, walk distance and duration, and workout logs.

Location and walking

Walk tracking uses foreground location while the app is open. The app uses location points to estimate distance during a session; it saves distance and duration, not the GPS route points.

AI features and photos

If you choose an AI feature, the relevant request and selected tracker summary are sent through the DIGITracker backend to its AI provider. GPS coordinates are not sent to AI. In the web app, an attached JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo is held in device memory and sent only when you submit the AI request; the app does not save that photo to your account.

Account controls

You can request account deletion from Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account. The app documentation says this removes your account, synced tracker data, active sessions, and stored AI quota records. Data export is available for wellness notes.

Before this policy is final

We still need to publish the company’s legal identity, contact address, effective date, website analytics and hosting details, service-provider retention details, and the process for privacy requests. Please contact HideOut Creatives through the Contacts page once the public contact channel is available.

This draft is not legal advice and should be reviewed against the deployed product and applicable law before it is treated as the final privacy policy.
