PRIVACY

We take personal information seriously.

This draft explains the DIGITracker data practices currently described in its product documentation. Confirm it against the live service and complete the company details before relying on it as the final policy.

WORKING DRAFT · MUST BE REVIEWED BEFORE PUBLICATION

Who this policy concerns

This draft covers the HideOut Creatives company website and DIGITracker. It should identify the legal entity responsible for processing, the applicable contact address, and the date it takes effect before publication.

Information DIGITracker handles

To provide the tracker, a signed-in account may store account information and entries users choose to add. These entries can include activities and events, meal notes, body measurements, sleep and soreness check-ins, walk distance and duration, and exercise and workout logs. Wellness notes sync with the account; the product documentation says users can export them.

Location during a walk

Walk tracking uses foreground location while the app is open to estimate distance. DIGITracker saves the distance and duration of a walk, not the GPS route points. The documentation says walk coordinates are not sent to AI features.

AI requests and images

When a user chooses an AI feature, the relevant request and selected tracker summary are sent through the DIGITracker backend to the configured AI provider so it can generate a response. Users should avoid including information they do not want processed for that request.

In the web app, a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image attached to an AI request is held in device memory and sent when the user submits the request. The product documentation says the image is not saved to the user’s account. The live provider’s handling and retention terms must be checked and linked here before publication.

Account deletion

Users can request deletion from Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account. The current product documentation says this deletes the account, synced tracker data, active sessions, and stored AI quota records. Confirm whether backups, security logs, and third-party records have separate retention periods.

Website and service providers

The website records aggregated page views and clicks on the DIGITracker APK download. These records contain the page path, event type, date, and count; they do not store a visitor account, GPS data, or IP address. Website event aggregates are automatically deleted after 90 days. The temporary request limiter uses the connecting IP address in server memory and does not save it to the analytics database. The website feedback widgets do not submit ratings to a server or store them on this site. Selecting “Share rating” opens a prefilled email draft; the rating and optional comment are shared only if the visitor sends that email. The company website is hosted on Vercel. The DIGITracker API is hosted on Render, stores tracker data using MongoDB, and routes requested AI tasks through OpenRouter. Google is used for Google sign-in. Each provider may process technical information needed to operate its service. Confirm provider retention and international processing terms, and how a person may exercise privacy rights, before treating this draft as final.

Contact and legal review

For privacy questions, contact shadowhacktech@gmail.com. This is a product-practice summary, not legal advice. Have a qualified reviewer confirm the data flows, company identity, retention, user rights, and applicable law before treating this as the final policy.
