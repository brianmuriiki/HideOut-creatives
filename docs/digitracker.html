APP GUIDE / HC—001

DIGITracker, explained.

A practical guide to the app’s planning, health, movement, and gym features.

OverviewAccountPlanningHealth and walksGym trackingAI featuresInstall and updateData and privacy

Overview

DIGITracker brings activity planning, wellness notes, walk summaries, and gym logging together in one personal tracker. It is available as an Android APK and as a web app.

Account and sign-in

An account is required to use the tracker. Sign in with Google or use the account options available in the app. Your account keeps synced tracker data available across supported sessions.

Planning your day

Add activities and events to organize your day. AI can help draft a plan when you ask; review the suggestions and adjust them to your needs.

Health notes and walk tracking

Record meal and food-group notes, body metrics, sleep and soreness check-ins, and walk sessions. Walk tracking requests foreground location and runs while the app is open. It saves estimated distance and duration, not GPS route points.

Health and fitness notes are personal tracking aids, not medical assessments.

Gym tracking

Log exercises and sets with reps, weight, and effort (RPE). Review workout history and use the available rest timer, exercise cues, substitutions, estimated personal records, and weekly volume summaries to understand your training.

Workout suggestions are optional and should be adapted to your experience and how you feel. Stop if you experience pain, dizziness, or feel unwell.

AI suggestions and photos

AI features can help with daily plans, wellness ideas, natural-language workout logging, and workout summaries. Your request and relevant summary are sent only when you choose the AI feature. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect; review them before acting.

On the web app, you can attach or take a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 2 MB for an AI request. It stays in device memory and is sent only when you submit; the app does not save it to your account.

Install and update

On Android, download the APK and open it. Android may ask you to allow your browser to install apps from unknown sources. Confirm the installation prompt. Updates to an installed APK require downloading the newer signed build and confirming its installation. Some compatible JavaScript and asset changes may arrive through in-app updates.

Data and privacy

GPS coordinates are not included in AI requests. Account deletion is available in Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account. See the Privacy page for the current working summary.
